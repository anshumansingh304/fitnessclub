Gym Diet Goals

Fuel the body you are actually training for.

Bulk

Build Size

Eat a controlled calorie surplus, prioritize protein, and keep carbs high enough to train hard. Focus on rice, oats, potatoes, eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, dal, nuts, olive oil, and milk.

  • Target 10-20% above maintenance calories.
  • Gain slowly to limit unnecessary fat gain.
  • Pair surplus eating with progressive strength training.
Weight Loss

Cut Fat

Create a steady calorie deficit without starving yourself. Build meals around lean protein, vegetables, fruit, dal, curd, eggs, grilled meats, tofu, and whole grains.

  • A moderate deficit is easier to sustain.
  • Protein and fiber help control hunger.
  • Limit sugary drinks, fried snacks, and ultra-processed foods.
Lean

Stay Athletic

Eat near maintenance or in a small deficit/surplus based on progress. Keep protein consistent, time carbs around workouts, and use healthy fats for hormones and recovery.

  • Choose mostly whole foods and repeatable meals.
  • Use strength training to preserve muscle.
  • Adjust calories every 2-3 weeks based on progress.

Nutrition guidance summarized from Healthline references on meal planning, bulking, cutting, and healthy weight gain.
